# deck-cycle

A cascaded deck; the front card swipes off and tucks in at the back.

- **Rig:** `stack` (cycle) · **camera:** `front` · **cards:** 4-6 · **loop:** yes · **length:** 8s (5-14s)
- **Type:** front, up to 5 words
- **From:** Creator cards

**What it's for:** profiles or team cards; UI cards or features one at a time; options to compare.

**Avoid:** more than 6 cards (each gets too little time).

**What breaks it:** Many cards in a short duration turn the cycle into flicking.

**Beats:**
- `cycle` → `rig.peel` in N steps: hands the focus to the next card: the front one swipes away and tucks behind
- `orbit-out` → `camera.orbit`: keeps the frame alive: the deck turns slightly
- `orbit-back` → `camera.orbit`: keeps the frame alive: the deck turns slightly
