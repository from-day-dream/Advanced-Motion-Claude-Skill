# fan-spread

Cards fan open from a pivot, settle, and breathe.

- **Rig:** `arc` (fan) · **camera:** `front` · **cards:** 3-7 · **loop:** no · **length:** 6s (4-10s)
- **Type:** front, up to 5 words
- **From:** fills a gap the references leave

**What it's for:** a small set of options; a hand of cards; a quick reveal.

**Avoid:** more than 7 cards; loops.

**What breaks it:** Many cards overlap at the pivot; wide spreads push the outer cards off frame.

**Beats:**
- `open` → `rig.open`: fans the set open from one point so every card gets seen
- `breathe-in` → `rig.open`: keeps the frame alive: the fan breathes
- `breathe-out` → `rig.open`: keeps the frame alive: the fan breathes
