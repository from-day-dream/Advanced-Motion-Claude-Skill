# peel-stack

A deck seen from below in strong perspective; the front card lifts away and the next is revealed.

- **Rig:** `stack` (peel) · **camera:** `below` · **cards:** 6-12 · **loop:** yes · **length:** 10s (6-16s)
- **Type:** none or front, up to 5 words
- **From:** Colour stack

**What it's for:** a colour or product range; a sequence revealed one by one; before/after or step reveals.

**Avoid:** fewer than 6 cards; more than one line of type.

**What breaks it:** Short stacks peel too fast to read; long durations with few cards make each peel sluggish.

**Beats:**
- `build-in` → `rig.build`: reveals the cards: they arrive in order before the motion takes over (dropped when the scene loops)
- `peel` → `rig.peel` in N steps: reveals the next card: each lift uncovers what was beneath
