# depth-corridor

Parallel planes seen at three-quarters; the camera trucks along them while the far planes frost.

- **Rig:** `stack` (corridor) · **camera:** `front` · **cards:** 8-14 · **loop:** yes · **length:** 10s (6-16s)
- **Type:** front, up to 5 words
- **From:** Unveil

**What it's for:** a timeline or archive; case studies in a row; a calm, premium walk-through.

**Avoid:** square-on readable cards (they sit at an angle).

**What breaks it:** Changing corridorGap without changing the travel value breaks the loop (travel must be N × gap).

**Beats:**
- `build-in` → `rig.build`: reveals the cards: they arrive in order before the motion takes over (dropped when the scene loops)
- `truck` → `rig.travel`: carries the viewer along the row, plane after plane
