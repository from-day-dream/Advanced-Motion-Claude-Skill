# poster-ring

Bent posters on a cylinder around the camera, spinning constantly.

- **Rig:** `ring` (cylinder) · **camera:** `inside-axis` · **cards:** 8-16 · **loop:** yes · **length:** 12s (8-20s)
- **Type:** none or front, up to 4 words
- **From:** VK Fest lineup

**What it's for:** a lineup; a poster series; a product range; event speakers.

**Avoid:** fewer than 8 items (the ring shows its gaps); dense text on cards.

**What breaks it:** Under 8 posters the ring looks sparse; dense text blurs as it turns.

**Beats:**
- `build-in` → `rig.build`: reveals the cards: they arrive in order before the motion takes over (dropped when the scene loops)
- `spin` → `rig.angle`: carries the lineup past the viewer, poster after poster
