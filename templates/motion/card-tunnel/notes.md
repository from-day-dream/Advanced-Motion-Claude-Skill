# card-tunnel

Cards line a tube; the camera flies forward through it toward the word.

- **Rig:** `ring` (tunnel) · **camera:** `inside-axis` · **cards:** 20-56 · **loop:** yes · **length:** 10s (6-16s)
- **Type:** front, up to 3 words
- **From:** fills a gap the references leave

**What it's for:** an immersive intro; a big archive; a destination word.

**Avoid:** few cards (rings repeat); readable cards.

**What breaks it:** Changing rings or spacing without the travel value breaks the loop (travel = rings × spacing).

**Beats:**
- `fly` → `rig.travel`: carries the viewer forward through the work toward the message
