# tilted-wall

A large grid on a plane tilted about 40°, scrolling diagonally forever.

- **Rig:** `wall` · **camera:** `front` · **cards:** 20-40 · **loop:** yes · **length:** 14s (8-20s)
- **Type:** front, up to 4 words
- **From:** fills a gap the references leave

**What it's for:** a big body of work behind one line; a catalogue; social proof.

**Avoid:** fewer than 20 cards (repeats show).

**What breaks it:** A short duration with many rows scrolls too fast; few cards repeat visibly.

**Beats:**
- `build-in` → `rig.build`: reveals the cards: they arrive in order before the motion takes over (dropped when the scene loops)
- `scroll` → `rig.scrollY`: keeps the wall moving: an endless body of work behind the line
