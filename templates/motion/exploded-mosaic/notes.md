# exploded-mosaic

One image split into tiles separates into depth, turns, and reassembles.

- **Rig:** `mosaic` · **camera:** `front` · **cards:** 1-1 · **loop:** yes · **length:** 8s (5-12s)
- **Type:** front, up to 5 words
- **From:** fills a gap the references leave

**What it's for:** one hero image; a product shot; a reveal of a single visual.

**Avoid:** more than one card; images with small text.

**What breaks it:** Small text on the image breaks across tiles; very deep explodes leave the frame.

**Beats:**
- `explode` → `rig.explode`: breaks the image apart to show what it is made of
- `reassemble` → `rig.explode`: reassembles it: the image is whole again under the line
- `orbit-out` → `camera.orbit`: connects the tiles in depth: the view turns to show the separation
- `orbit-back` → `camera.orbit`: connects the tiles in depth: the view turns to show the separation
