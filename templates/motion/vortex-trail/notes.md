# vortex-trail

Images stream out of a point, growing as they spiral toward the camera.

- **Rig:** `spine` (spiral) · **camera:** `front` · **cards:** 20-40 · **loop:** yes · **length:** 10s (6-15s)
- **Type:** interleaved or front, up to 2 words
- **From:** Cursor trail

**What it's for:** an archive or a big set of work; energy, abundance; a reveal word at the centre.

**Avoid:** fewer than 20 cards; readable cards.

**What breaks it:** Few cards make the stream sparse and the spiral obvious.

**Beats:**
- `stream` → `rig.offset`: carries the images out of the centre toward the viewer, in an endless stream
